Who We Are

An Integrated Building Solutions Company, Reimagined

Bridging the gap between the world's most respected building material brands, advanced waterproofing solutions, bespoke luxury wellness experiences, and India's most ambitious developers, architects and homeowners.

Our Story

Built on Trust, Delivered with Distinction

Maximus Buildcon began with a single conviction — that India's design ambitions deserved unrestricted access to the world's finest building materials. Today, we are an integrated building solutions company with a network of over 400 retail partners across Maharashtra and Gujarat.

Alongside premium building materials, we deliver fully integrated waterproofing solutions — from system specification and product supply to expert application and warranty-backed execution.

Through Fahrenheit Wellness, we create bespoke luxury wellness spaces, bringing world-class wellness experiences to residential, hospitality and commercial projects.

From landmark developments to luxury residences and large-scale housing projects, our products, services and expertise help shape the way India is built.

Our success is built on a simple philosophy: represent the best, deliver with excellence, and support every partner with technical expertise and dependable service.

What We Do

End-to-End Building Solutions

We do more than supply products. We help architects specify the right solutions, support developers across large-scale projects, deliver fully integrated waterproofing services from supply to application with warranty-backed assurance, design bespoke luxury wellness spaces, train retail partners, and maintain inventory to meet India's project timelines.

  • Premium building materials and bathware distribution
  • Fully integrated waterproofing solutions — supply, application and warranty
  • Bespoke luxury wellness solutions
  • Direct project supply to India's leading developers
  • 400+ retail partner network across Maharashtra and Gujarat
  • Specification consulting for architects and designers
  • Two showrooms — Mumbai and Vadodara
