Waterproofing · Supply & Application

Greentek Plast

A unique and revolutionary waterproofing solution that provides long-lasting protection for all types of construction projects.

Protection That Outlasts the Build

Water is the single most persistent threat to a structure's lifespan. Greentek Plast systems are engineered to hold the line — below ground, behind walls, under terraces and around water-retaining structures. As a supply and application company, Maximus Buildcon specifies the right system, supplies the material, and executes the work with warranty-backed assurance.
