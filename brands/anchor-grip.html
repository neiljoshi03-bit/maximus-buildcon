Greentek Plast · Waterproofing System

Anchor Grip

Below-grade and water-retaining waterproofing — engineered for structures under sustained hydrostatic pressure, where failure is expensive and access for repair is limited.

Where the Water Pushes Back

Below ground, water does not simply sit — it presses. Anchor Grip is the Greentek Plast system specified where that pressure is constant: basements that sit below the water table, retaining walls holding back saturated soil, and the tanks and pools that are designed to hold water in rather than keep it out. It is the part of the building nobody sees and nobody can easily reach once the structure is complete, which is precisely why it is worth specifying correctly the first time.

Applications

Specified For

Four conditions where Anchor Grip is the system of choice.

01

Basement

Full below-grade tanking for parking levels, service floors and habitable basements — protecting against both groundwater and lateral seepage.

02

Retention Wall

Earth-retaining structures where saturated backfill exerts continuous pressure against the wall face for the life of the building.

03

Underground Storage Tank

Water-retaining structures where the system must perform in both directions — holding the stored water in and keeping groundwater out.

04

Swimming Pool

Pool shells and surrounds, where a small failure becomes a visible, continuous loss and a costly excavation to correct.
