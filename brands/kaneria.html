Construction Materials · Supply

KANERIA

High-quality KANERIA construction materials — dry mix plaster, AAC blocks and floor screed, supplied to project timelines across Maharashtra and Gujarat.

The Substrate Everything Sits On

Finishes get specified carefully. What sits underneath them often does not — and that is where tolerances drift, screeds crack and plaster debonds. Alongside our waterproofing systems, Maximus Buildcon supplies KANERIA construction materials so the substrate is held to the same standard as the surface: consistent, factory-controlled, and delivered in the volumes a live site actually needs.

Product Range

Supplied Materials

Three core products, available for project-scale supply.

01

Dry Mix Plaster

Factory-Blended

Pre-blended plaster delivered ready for water addition — removing the site-mixing variability that causes uneven coverage, shrinkage cracking and debonding on large elevations.

  • Consistent mix from batch to batch
  • Reduced site wastage and labour
  • Suited to high-volume application
02

AAC Blocks

Autoclaved Aerated Concrete

Lightweight masonry blocks that reduce dead load on the structure while improving thermal performance — a standard specification on modern high-rise and large-format developments.

  • Lower structural dead load
  • Thermal and acoustic performance
  • Dimensional accuracy, faster walling
03

Floor Screed

Levelling & Preparation

The layer that decides whether a premium tile lays flat. Controlled screed gives the flatness and strength that large-format porcelain and stone finishes demand.

  • Flat, true substrate for tiling
  • Consistent strength and thickness
  • Prepared for large-format finishes
