Waterproofing

A full-service waterproofing division — system specification, material supply, and expert application backed by warranty. Not a product we sell, but a result we deliver.

Supply and Application

Most waterproofing failures are not product failures — they are application failures. Maximus Buildcon is a supply and application company: we assess the structure, specify the system that suits the condition, deliver the material to your programme, and execute the work with trained teams. Because we own both halves, we can stand behind the finished system rather than one part of it.

Our Process

Specified, Supplied, Applied

Four stages, one accountable party.

01

Site Assessment

We look at the structure, the water source and the exposure before recommending anything — because the right system depends on the condition, not the catalogue.

02

System Specification

A written specification the contractor can build to — surface preparation, product, coverage rates, detailing at junctions and curing requirements.

03

Material Supply

Full range held and delivered to your site programme, including the ancillary products the detail actually requires.

04

Warranty-Backed Application

Trained application teams execute the work to specification, and the completed system carries our warranty.
