Greentek Plast · Waterproofing System

Geo Proof Materials

The broadest of the Greentek Plast systems — covering wet areas, terraces, podiums and facades, the everyday surfaces where most buildings begin to leak.

The Surfaces That Fail First

Very few buildings leak from the foundation. They leak from a bathroom sunk slab, a terrace that ponds after monsoon, a flower bed that was never detailed, or an exterior wall that absorbs driven rain. Geo Proof Materials cover that entire everyday territory — the wet areas, horizontal surfaces and exposed elevations where waterproofing is most often value-engineered out and most often regretted.

Where It Counts

Detailing, Not Just Coating

A membrane is only as good as the junctions it is taken into.

01

Wet Areas

Toilets and bathrooms — sunk slabs, drain outlets and wall junctions treated as a continuous system rather than a coated floor.

02

Terraces & Balconies

Exposed horizontal surfaces that take direct monsoon loading, thermal movement and standing water at the parapet.

03

Podiums & Flower Beds

Landscaped decks over occupied space — where soil holds moisture permanently against the structure below.

04

Overhead Water Tanks

Water-retaining structures at height, where a slow leak tracks down through the building before it is ever noticed.

05

Exterior Wall Surfaces

Facade treatment against driven rain and capillary absorption — the cause of most unexplained internal damp patches.

06

Interior Brick Work

Internal masonry treatment where moisture migration between wet and dry areas needs to be interrupted at the wall.
