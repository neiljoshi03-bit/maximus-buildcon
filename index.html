About Maximus Buildcon

Integrated Building Solutions, Reimagined

Maximus Buildcon is an integrated building materials supplier, a full-service waterproofing company, and a bespoke luxury wellness solutions provider. With a network spanning over 400 retailers across Maharashtra and Gujarat, we partner with India's most respected developers, architects, and contractors — bridging the gap between world-class brands and landmark Indian projects.

Our portfolio caters to the full spectrum — from exclusive architect-designed homes and luxury developments to large-scale housing projects — delivering premium products, specialized expertise, and end-to-end solutions at every scale.
